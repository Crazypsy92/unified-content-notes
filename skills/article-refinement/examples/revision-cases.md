# Revision Cases

These are original teaching examples, not excerpts from a student's paper.

## Case 1 — Remove an empty opening

**Before**

随着相关技术的快速发展，该领域逐渐受到越来越多研究人员的关注，具有重要的理论意义和实践价值。

**After**

该领域的研究主要关注传感器输出稳定性及其在实际工况下的能量利用问题。

**Why**

The revised sentence names a research focus. Use it only when the following section actually discusses these issues; otherwise ask the author to supply the specific focus.

## Case 2 — Keep a plan as a plan

**Before**

本课题将通过实验验证该方案能够显著提高系统效率。

**After**

本课题拟通过实验测量系统输出，并分析该方案对系统效率的影响。

**Why**

The original states an unverified result as certain. The revision restores the planned status and avoids promising a positive outcome.

## Case 3 — Preserve a technical parameter

**Before**

实验计划采用 10 Hz 的周期载荷，记录输出电压随时间的变化。

**After**

实验计划采用 10 Hz 的周期载荷，记录输出电压随时间的变化。

**Why**

The sentence is already precise. The correct edit is no edit. The number is illustrative and must not be reused as a real experimental parameter.

## Case 4 — Flag a missing fact instead of inventing it

**Before**

实验采用实验室提供的样品进行测试。

**Review note**

请作者确认样品的实际来源、型号和数量。若来源并非实验室提供，不应保留该表述。

**Why**

A plausible source is not evidence. The editor must not infer laboratory resources or sample provenance.
